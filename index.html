Privacy Policy: Delhi Driving Simulator 3D

Effective date: 13 November 2026

Delhi Driving Simulator 3D ("the game") is published by Holly Films ("we"). This policy explains what information the game handles.

Contact: hello@hollyfilms.com

Summary

  • The game has no advertising and no analytics or tracking software. A future version may include advertising; this policy will be updated before that version is released.
  • We do not run any servers for the game, and we do not collect, store or sell your personal information.
  • The game can be played without signing in to anything.

Information the game handles

Game progress. Your progress (vehicles, coins, missions, settings) is saved in an encrypted file on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Google Play Games (optional). If you sign in with Google Play Games, the game stores a copy of your progress in your own Google account (Saved Games) so it can be restored on another device, and it reads your Play Games player ID and display name to show your profile, achievements and leaderboards. This information is held by Google under Google's privacy policy. We do not receive or keep a copy.

Purchases (optional). In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. The game receives only a confirmation of the purchase from Google Play so it can give you the item, and keeps that confirmation in your save file so an item is not granted twice.

Crash and performance reports. If you have agreed to share diagnostics with Google on your device, Google Play may give us anonymous, combined crash and performance statistics. They do not identify you.

Information we do not collect

The game does not ask for or use your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, phone number or advertising ID.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

  • On your device: uninstall the game, or clear its data in Android Settings.
  • In your Google account: open the Play Games app, go to Settings, then "Delete Play Games account and data", and choose this game.

Third-party services

  • Google Play Games Services and Google Play Billing: https://policies.google.com/privacy
